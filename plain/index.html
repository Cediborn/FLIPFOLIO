01 About

Great software starts when it feels tested in reality.

I’m Cedi, a first-year Computer Science student at the University of Ghana who spends most of his time turning ideas into working software. Most of what I know came from building things I actually wanted to use — then paying attention to what broke.

Right now I’m exploring web development and game development by shipping small, real things: a football game that runs in the browser, a budget that fits student life, a discovery tool that filters by what you can afford.

  • Based inAccra, Ghana
  • StudyingBSc CS · Level 100
  • ApproachLearn by shipping
